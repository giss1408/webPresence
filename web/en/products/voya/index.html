Voya

A seat-booking app for transport operators, with boarding tickets that can be verified offline.

Ask about this product All products

Preview

The problem it solves

Transport operators still sell seats at the counter or by phone: no real-time view of free seats, double sales, and tickets that are easy to forge.

What is included

  • Rider app: book a seat without creating an account
  • Seats held for a short time while paying, to prevent double bookings
  • Payment by mobile money or card, or cash on boarding
  • Boarding ticket with a signed QR code, verifiable offline
  • Fleet app for operators: vehicles, routes, trips and passenger lists
  • Platform administration: operators, subscriptions and staff permissions
