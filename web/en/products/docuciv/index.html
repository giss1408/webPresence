DocuCiv

A platform to request, process and track administrative documents online, between citizens, administrations and businesses.

Ask about this product All products

The problem it solves

Getting a certificate means travelling, queueing and having no idea how your request is progressing.

What is included

  • Online request with the reference of the document concerned
  • Supporting document sent from the phone and checked on receipt
  • Review queue for agents of the issuing administration
  • Step-by-step request tracking with notifications
  • Complaints from both the citizen and the administration
  • Citizen app, administration portal and platform owner back office
